abstract: "We explore resonances and metastable states in non-relativistic quantum electrodynamics. When N bosonic particles interact with a free radiation field, their stable excited states dissolve into the continuous spectrum and become decaying metastable states. We use complex dilation (spectral deformation) to analytically bypass the spectrum's branch cut and expose these resonance poles. Using a perturbative expansion and then the Feshbach map, we derive the states' second-order energy shifts and exponential decay rates, showing they align with Fermi's golden rule."
