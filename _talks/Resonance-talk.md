### Abstract

We explore resonances and metastable states in non-relativistic quantum electrodynamics. When \(N\) bosonic particles interact with a free radiation field, stable excited states may dissolve into the continuous spectrum and become decaying metastable states.

Using complex dilation, also known as spectral deformation, we analytically continue across the branch cut of the continuous spectrum and reveal the associated resonance poles. We then combine perturbation theory with the Feshbach map to derive the second-order energy shifts and exponential decay rates. These decay rates are shown to agree with Fermi’s golden rule.
