\section{Research interests}
\cvitem{}{Mathematical physics and analysis of nonlinear PDEs; scaling and mean-field limits of
  quantum dynamical systems; spectral and dynamical problems in quantum field theory;
  coupled particle--radiation dynamics, nonlinear resonance cascades, and radiative
  Bose--Einstein condensation. Current research interests also include entropy vanishing,
  purification of quantum states, and local well-posedness for nonlinear dispersive equations.}
